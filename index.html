Your material stays yours

Nothing to learn before you start, and nothing to lose when the class ends.

  • Plain files, not a database Export it, back it up, hand it to next year's class. You still have it after the semester ends, in a format anything can read.
  • Works on a phone, on bad lab wifi No app to install, no account to make. It loads over a weak connection and costs nothing to host.
  • Answers accept how people actually write A question can take several correct phrasings, and near-misses earn partial credit — because "tunica externa" spelled slightly wrong is not the same as not knowing it.
